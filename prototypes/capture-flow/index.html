Capture-flow prototype — “Camera chooses and shows the plan”

Throwaway discussion prototype for Explore capture and explanation interactions. One file, nothing installed, no data leaves this page.

The question this demo answers: does the capture-plan interaction feel right — the plan appearing before the shutter, how it adapts to conditions and priorities, what guidance appears during capture, what the explanation admits afterwards, and how the two after-capture workflows differ? Drive the buttons and react to anything that feels wrong.

SIMULATED Everything about the scene (brightness, movement, faces) is simulated — the S22 probe measured capture and file behavior, never scene recognition. Numbers about exposures, files and trade-offs come from measured S22 evidence (2026-10-06/07) and are marked [measured].
