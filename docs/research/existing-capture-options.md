# Existing capture options on the Galaxy S25 Ultra

Research date: 2026-10-06. This is a documented baseline for comparing Samsung Camera, Expert RAW and Open Camera. No phone, installed app versions or sample captures were available. Nothing here establishes quality or compatibility on the owner's particular S25 Ultra.

## What Samsung saves

| Capture path | Documented behavior | What it establishes for this comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Samsung Camera Photo | Automatic settings and a rendered photograph. Samsung says its standard app decides color, tone, detail and noise; Photo offers quality-versus-speed optimization. | A useful everyday output baseline for rear scenes and selfies. Compare its finished output; separate source exposures were not documented. |
| Samsung Camera Pro RAW | Samsung describes Pro as 12-bit single-frame RAW, in contrast to Expert RAW. | A baseline for developing one sensor exposure yourself. Confirm DNG output and available cameras on the installed version. |
| Expert RAW | Samsung describes 16-bit Linear DNG and JPEG output with multi-frame HDR and noise processing. | A processed RAW baseline. Its saved DNG is not a set of separate source exposures. |

Sources: Samsung's [camera engineering interview, 2022-04-14](https://news.samsung.com/in/experts-talk-collaborating-on-the-galaxy-s22-series-expert-raw-app), [camera settings support](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001353/) and [Expert RAW CamCyclopedia, published 2026-06-30](https://r2.community.samsung.com/t5/CamCyclopedia/Expert-RAW/ba-p/22441198). The last article explicitly dates its feature list to February 2026 and warns that support varies by model and software. Samsung identifies CamCyclopedia as its [official camera encyclopedia](https://r2.community.samsung.com/t5/Tech-Talk/Launching-CamCyclopedia-guides-for-Galaxy-smartphone-users/td-p/13049583).

The 12-bit and 16-bit descriptions are Samsung's output descriptions. They do not establish effective sensor precision, usable dynamic range or a 16-stop capture range. Nor does a `.dng` extension establish that an image is a single unprocessed exposure. Inspect the actual files before comparing recoverable highlights, shadows and noise.

### Does Expert RAW offer traditional AEB with separate DNG exposures?

**No such feature was verified in the reviewed Samsung documentation.** Treat availability on the owner's phone as unresolved, rather than recording a categorical absence.

Samsung's [Multiple exposure guide](https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/professional-photography-with-galaxy-expert-raw-app/) describes a combined image, with manual or continuous shooting. The [official Multi Exposures article, 2023-01-30](https://r2.community.samsung.com/t5/CamCyclopedia/Multi-Exposures/ba-p/13245898) documents 2 to 9 shots and Add, Average, Bright and Dark overlays. Continuous means shots at fixed intervals; it does not document automatically changing exposure by a configured EV bracket or exporting each source DNG. The June 2026 [Expert RAW article](https://r2.community.samsung.com/t5/CamCyclopedia/Expert-RAW/ba-p/22441198) still describes Multi Exposures as a composite and lists no separate-file AEB control. Samsung's [S25 series guide, 2025-01-22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_H0AO4ELMck) also presents Multiple exposure as creative blending.

This search cannot prove the feature is absent from every release, Labs configuration or regional installation. An EV adjustment, repeated shots, internal multi-frame HDR, and Multiple exposure are different from one shutter action producing a configured bracket of separate DNGs. Check the installed app's controls and saved files to settle that distinction. Generic regional guides and old screenshots are not a complete inventory of current S25 Ultra behavior.

## Open Camera's fixed bracket

The project [history](https://opencamera.org.uk/history.html) currently lists version 1.56.2, dated 2026-05-27. Source inspection used the project's SourceForge Git repository at commit `0dd4cbe78872df2c6e4eb6cee3fb0d5637b0f52e`, tagged `v1.56.2`; the manifest reports the same version. This establishes the inspected release, not the version installed on the owner's phone.

- Exposure Bracketing saves source exposures for processing elsewhere. Its fixed controls offer 3 or 5 images and an outer span of 0.5, 1, 2 or 3 stops. The stops setting is the distance from the base to either extreme, not necessarily the gap between adjacent shots. With a 2-stop span, 3 images target `[-2, 0, +2]`; 5 target `[-2, -1, 0, +1, +2]`. These are configured brackets, not scene-selected bracket counts or spans. Sources: [help](https://opencamera.org.uk/help.html) and [release arrays](https://sourceforge.net/p/opencamera/code/ci/0dd4cbe78872df2c6e4eb6cee3fb0d5637b0f52e/tree/app/src/main/res/values/arrays.xml).
- The Camera2 bracket holds selected or current ISO within its reported range and varies exposure time around the selected or current base. It clamps dark and bright requests to exposure-time limits. Consequently, requested EV separation can shrink at a limit; verify capture metadata. Source: [CameraController2, capability checks and `takePictureBurstBracketing`](https://sourceforge.net/p/opencamera/code/ci/0dd4cbe78872df2c6e4eb6cee3fb0d5637b0f52e/tree/app/src/main/java/net/sourceforge/opencamera/cameracontroller/CameraController2.java).
- RAW bracketing requires Camera2, RAW enabled, "Allow RAW for expo bracketing" enabled, and the app's RAW-burst eligibility. The inspected source gates that eligibility on at least 512 MB of Android large-heap allowance, which is not total device RAM. It saves the RAW burst as individual files without merging them. Sources: [MyApplicationInterface, `isRawAllowed` and `onRawBurstPictureTaken`](https://sourceforge.net/p/opencamera/code/ci/0dd4cbe78872df2c6e4eb6cee3fb0d5637b0f52e/tree/app/src/main/java/net/sourceforge/opencamera/MyApplicationInterface.java) and [MainActivity, `supportsBurstRaw`](https://sourceforge.net/p/opencamera/code/ci/0dd4cbe78872df2c6e4eb6cee3fb0d5637b0f52e/tree/app/src/main/java/net/sourceforge/opencamera/MainActivity.java).
- For each camera, RAW requires the reported Camera2 RAW capability and a usable `RAW_SENSOR` output size. Camera2 AEB requires at least LIMITED hardware support plus sensitivity and exposure-time ranges. The implementation deliberately does not require the `MANUAL_SENSOR` capability flag. Source: [CameraController2, `getCameraFeatures`](https://sourceforge.net/p/opencamera/code/ci/0dd4cbe78872df2c6e4eb6cee3fb0d5637b0f52e/tree/app/src/main/java/net/sourceforge/opencamera/cameracontroller/CameraController2.java).

The [help](https://opencamera.org.uk/help.html) distinguishes Expo from HDR mode. HDR combines images; saving its base images, including eligible RAW files, requires "Save all images for HDR mode". RAW-only saving requires Android 7; current Open Camera requires [Android 6 or newer](https://opencamera.org.uk/). Some phones restrict third-party lens access, and manufacturer Camera2 implementations can fail despite advertised support. Neither Samsung Camera's private processing nor all its resolutions and lenses are guaranteed in Open Camera. Check the [project compatibility notes](https://opencamera.org.uk/devices.html) and actual files.

The project states that Open Camera is licensed under [GPL v3 or later](https://opencamera.org.uk/). This note records that fact only.

## People, group selfies and scenery

Samsung Photo should be included in every comparison because the required result includes people and group selfies, not only stationary rear-camera scenery. Samsung documents [palm and voice capture, timer and selfie orientation](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001353/); Scene optimizer is rear-camera only. A timer helps capture ergonomics but does not establish RAW availability.

No reviewed evidence guarantees front-camera RAW or RAW AEB on this S25 Ultra. The newer Expert RAW documentation includes a selfie setting, but that is not proof that this model and installed version export front-camera DNGs. Open Camera's camera capability checks likewise apply to the selected camera, not the phone as a whole.

For people, measure missed expressions, blinking, subject blur and ghosting as well as highlight recovery. Open Camera explicitly warns that [HDR movement handling is imperfect](https://opencamera.org.uk/help.html). Testing a still scene cannot establish that a multi-frame capture will work well for a group selfie. These are evaluation priorities, not measured defects in the owner's apps.

## Owner's comparison checklist

1. Record exact S25 Ultra model and region, Android/One UI build, Samsung Camera, Camera Assistant if present, Expert RAW and Open Camera versions. Capture the visible settings, Labs options, output format and resolution. In Open Camera, record Camera API and copy About diagnostics for each accessible rear and front camera, including RAW, exposure-time and bracket support.
2. Check Expert RAW for an explicit bracket count, EV spacing and separate-source save setting. Trigger one capture on a stationary scene. Count actual exported DNG files, inspect their exposure times and ISO, and distinguish the saved composite from any sources. A Gallery thumbnail or embedded DNG preview is insufficient. Record "control absent in this version" separately from "control present but files not produced".
3. Capture the same rear-camera scene in Photo, Pro RAW, Expert RAW, Open Camera Standard and Open Camera Expo with RAW where available. Start with the main rear camera, comparable framing and resolution, a stable support, flash off and unchanged lighting. Include a bright sky with dark foreground and an indoor scene with a bright window. Repeat handheld to test the intended use.
4. Repeat with people against a bright background, small natural movements and a front-camera group selfie. Include the normal Samsung Photo workflow even when RAW modes are unavailable. Record timer/remote operation, framing, orientation, focus, faces, capture delay and repeatability. Treat each unavailable mode as a capability result.
5. Preserve original files and settings per run. Confirm bracket count, DNG decoding, dimensions, lens identity, ISO and exposure-time separation. Compare rendered outputs as delivered; compare RAW development with recorded, consistent settings and identify any external merge. Score skin appearance, expressions, highlights, shadow noise, detail, halos, ghosting, delay and editing effort separately.
6. Record remaining gaps explicitly: installed feature inventory, front RAW availability, third-party lens/resolution access, exposure limits, actual bracket accuracy and preferred rendering. No current evidence selects a product or establishes that a custom capture pipeline improves these baselines.
