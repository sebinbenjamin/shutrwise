# Shutrwise photography

Shutrwise explores capture decisions and their explanations for beginner photographers.

## Language

**Capture plan**:
The proposed camera actions for a photograph or sequence of photographs, before they are taken.
_Avoid_: Capture explanation

**Capture explanation**:
An account of why particular capture choices suit the scene and the photographer's priorities.
_Avoid_: Capture plan

**Source capture**:
An original recorded photograph retained for later inspection or editing, separate from any image assembled from several captures.
_Avoid_: Generated photograph

**Capture feedback**:
Brief guidance about the scene, the capture plan, or the progress of a capture. A capture explanation gives the reasoning behind the choices.
