# Initial capture comparison

Working comparison method for Choose capture trials and evidence thresholds. [S22 technical measurements](../../../docs/research/s22-camera2-probe-20261006.md) establish RAW output and fixed exposure brackets. The [first RAW-versus-bracket experiment](../../../docs/research/raw-bracket-comparison-20261006.md) and a [fresh longer/lower-ISO single control](../../../docs/research/long-single-control-20261006.md) provide bounded source-data and qualitative shadow-render findings, with independent audits. A stronger single exposure was tested, but globally optimal settings and Samsung comparisons remain outstanding. This method investigates where the idea adds value before choosing a product direction. Scene coverage stays open.

## Agreed inputs

The audience is beginners. Explore useful captured data and clearer capture decisions as separate sources of value. Use a mix of handheld and tripod shooting. The working default is deliberate capture; quick capture is optional. Brief feedback precedes and accompanies capture, with deeper explanation afterward when wanted. Both immediate-photo and later-RAW-editing workflows remain open.

## Open scene coverage

Do not define a fixed list of supported scenes or an exclusive trial roster. Use whatever subjects and locations the owner wants to photograph. Scenic photography is an initial interest, not a scene restriction.

For comparisons, record the conditions that affect capture decisions: brightness and contrast, subject movement, camera stability, focus, the camera or lens used, and the photographer's priorities. Include handheld and tripod use over the course of exploration. New situations can add evidence at any time.

Compare alternative capture paths on the same scene with comparable framing and conditions. Record differences that cannot be controlled, especially changing light or subject motion. Measure selected camera paths separately; a rear-camera result does not establish front-camera RAW support. The goal is to discover useful behavior and limits, not to claim universal coverage from a few examples.

The owner reported varying light during the initial S22 captures. Treat those as technical smoke tests. A separate repeat dataset records the garage closed, lights on and phone fixed in place, confirmed by the owner. Use each repeat bracket's middle frame as its same-sequence single-RAW baseline. New runs meter and focus again, so their settings can differ. Artificial-light flicker and stability were not measured independently. Preserve these conditions beside the sources.

## Proposed baselines

Include Samsung Photo as the ordinary-camera reference. Where the owner's installed versions and selected camera permit it, compare Samsung Pro RAW, Expert RAW, one Open Camera RAW, and a fixed Open Camera RAW bracket. Record an unavailable mode as a capability result, not a failed photograph.

A custom capture engine or adaptive bracket is not required for this initial baseline. Commission a minimal probe only for a measurement the existing apps cannot provide. Later trials can add a custom or adaptive strategy if the initial evidence supports it.

## Keep the two evidence questions separate

For capture data, preserve source files and record settings and conditions. Compare the rendered photograph as delivered, and RAW editability under consistent, documented development settings. Assess highlights, shadows, useful detail, blur or ghosting, and whether the intended subject remains usable. Capture delay and extra editing effort count as costs. Additional files or longer waits are not evidence of better information.

For decision-making, use inexpensive written or simulated examples of the agreed feedback flow. Ask whether the beginner understands the capture plan, knows what to do while capturing, and can describe why a choice was made after opening the explanation. Mark feedback as simulated until it is driven by measured capture data. A clear explanation does not prove improved capture quality.

Retain quality, guidance, time, and editing-effort observations separately. Do not invent one combined score or assume that similar finished photographs imply identical RAW editability.

## Working continuation criteria

Continue a candidate direction when at least one source of value is meaningful and repeatable in a relevant situation: useful captured-data improvement, or guidance that makes the owner's capture decisions clearer and more dependable. Report costs and regressions alongside benefits; neither benefit excuses unreliable captures or lost source files.

Reconsider a candidate when the result is merely more files or elapsed time, when explanations are generic or misleading, or when practical costs outweigh either benefit. A benefit in one scene does not establish superiority across all photography.

The owner rejected a restricted scene list and tentatively accepted meaningful value in either captured data or decision-making as grounds for further exploration. This is an early qualitative criterion, not a final product acceptance threshold. Numerical thresholds and a practical shooting checklist can be refined once actual examples and available modes are known. No model, framework, final interface, or product direction is selected here.
