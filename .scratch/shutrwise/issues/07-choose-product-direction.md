# Choose the product direction and AI responsibilities

## Question

Given the device evidence and the owner's reaction to the experience alternatives, which product direction and core experience should Shutrwise pursue?

State the intended beginner, primary value, core capture and explanation flow, evidence that supports the choice, and unresolved limits. Decide which responsibilities need semantic intelligence and which can be handled by deterministic rules or conventional vision. AI is part of the exploration, but an LLM/VLM, cloud service, model architecture, commercial product, or full implementation specification is not preselected. Reconcile learning and usable capture data explicitly. The owner makes the decision in live discussion.
